Why vaccinate children?<\/h2>\t\t\t\t<\/div>\n\t\t\t\t<\/div>\n\t\t\t\t
\n\t\t\t\t
\n\t\t\t\t\t\t\t\t\t

Vaccines create immunity to a wide range of diseases, from the mild to the severe, some of which are not yet medically treatable. Some medical conditions are more viral than others, meaning that they are more or less contagious. Vaccination therefore helps prevent the transmission of diseases to our loved ones. The risk of contracting diseases is very high in the first year of a child’s life.<\/p>\t\t\t\t\t\t\t\t<\/div>\n\t\t\t\t<\/div>\n\t\t\t\t
