Interview with Dr. Dominique Tessier | Ramsay Hunt syndrome, a possible manifestation of shingles

  • Groupe Santé Voyage
  • June 29, 2022
  • Advice

Dr. Dominique Tessier, Medical Director of Groupe Santé Voyage and a specialist in family medicine, gave an interview to Radio-Canada to shed light on one of the possible manifestations of shingles, Ramsay Hunt syndrome. We’ve also seen popular singer Justin Bieber forced to postpone his North American tour following his diagnosis.

Statistics show that up to one in three people will develop an episode of shingles in their lifetime. The best way to prevent the disease is
immunization by vaccine.
 The Shingrix vaccine is over 90% safe and effective. Listen to the interview that sheds light on Ramsey Hunt syndrome and, more broadly, on shingles and the importance of vaccination.
