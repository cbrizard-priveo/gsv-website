General vaccination in clinics

Our general vaccination service is for anyone wishing to update their basic vaccination or benefit from seasonal flu vaccination.

It also targets those who would like to benefit from the protection of other vaccines such as shingles or HPV. Carried out within the professional framework that characterizes At our clinic , general vaccination is coordinated by nurses with an eye for detail. Adults, the elderly and young children all benefit from the expertise of our caring specialists.
