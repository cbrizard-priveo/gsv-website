Telemedicine consultation

Our Telemedicine consultation service lets you consult a healthcare professional from the comfort of your own home.

During this consultation you will receive :

  • An assessment of your itinerary and the risks to your health;
  • Health tips for your next trip;
  • Recommendations for personalized vaccines;
  • If required, a prescription can be sent directly to your pharmacy;

Following your telemedicine consultation, you may receive the appropriate vaccination at home or at one of our points of service.

  • Longueuil
  • Blainville
  • Pointe-Claire
