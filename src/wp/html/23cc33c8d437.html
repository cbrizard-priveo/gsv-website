Would you like to join a team that is a leader in its field? Are you a nurse or RAMQ non-participating physician interested in Travel Health and vaccination?

We are currently looking for a Nurse Technician

Your main responsibilities will be..:

  • Assess the health risks for the traveler, depending on the type of trip and the destination.
  • Inform, advise and vaccinate patients, according to age, conditions and/or habits, state of health and health risks based on destination and type of travel.
  • Draw up a vaccination schedule.
  • Assess and advise on the need for medication.
  • Approximately 20 hours a week. Schedules are subject to availability.
  • Main work locations Montreal & West Island

Skills required

  • Experience in vaccination
  • Experience in travel health is an asset
  • Good customer relations
  • Member in good standing of the OIIQ
  • Ability to travel (Corporate Services)
  • Bilingual (French & English)
