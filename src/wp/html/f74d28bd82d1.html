\n\t\t\t\t\t\t\t\t\tRome’s Colosseum comes directly from antiquity. It’s a legacy of the greatness and power of the Roman Empire. It has been a UNESCO World Heritage Site since 1980. Today, it is one of the 7 wonders of the modern world. The list was drawn up following a vote by Internet users in 2007. What do you need to know about this Italian tourist destination? \n

A building dating back to the Vespasian Empire <\/h2>\nThe Colosseum is located in the center of Rome. It was built in 70 AD. It was built during the reign of Emperor Vespasian. Its name used to be “Amphitheatrum Flavium”. It’s made of stone, brick and concrete. The monument was dedicated to major events. It can accommodate up to 50,000 people. It has several levels. In the past, spectators were seated according to their social status. \n

A place of brutality and cruelty<\/h2>\nThe Colosseum was primarily built to host intense, captivating shows. These include gladiatorial combat. These events were an integral part of popular culture at the time. Gladiators were generally criminals or slaves who fought in the arena to entertain the public. The monument also hosted wild animal hunts, where men faced off against animals such as lions and tigers. In addition, there were often human rituals.\n

The decline of the Colosseum<\/h2>\nThe Colosseum has been a huge success for centuries. Unfortunately, its original structure has been damaged over time. Earthquakes and looting were the main causes of its decline. Despite this, it continued to attract the attention of tourists. All the more so as it has been reused several times. For example, it served as a medieval fortification and stone quarry.\n

The Colosseum today<\/h2>\nRome’s Colosseum was restored during the Renaissance. Today, the site no longer hosts events. Nevertheless, it is one of Rome’s most popular attractions. Its architecture bears witness to ancient Roman engineering. The memories and mystical aura it contains. Its sturdiness and solidity. A rich historical heritage. The Colosseum has so much to offer visitors fascinated by Roman civilization. In fact, the site attracts up to 8 million tourists a year in this day and age. \n

The best time to visit the Colosseum <\/h2>\nAs we said, Rome’s Colosseum attracts a huge number of visitors. We therefore recommend that you avoid visiting during the high season. The place is packed. Schedule your trip between November and February, outside the Christmas vacations. So it’s best to plan your visit for the middle of the week. It’s also crowded at weekends. Finally, be on the scene as soon as possible. The site opens at 9am. \n
