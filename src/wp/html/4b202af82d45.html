About Travel Health Group and its clinics

Home > About us

At your service since 1997, Groupe Santé Voyage is a group of 5 service outlets that combine their efforts to offer you specialized travel health services and general vaccinations.

In addition, we offer telemedicine travel health consultations and the possibility of receiving your vaccinations at home.

Our founders

In 1997, with a view to creating a single point of contact for health-conscious international travellers, Dr. Martin Briard Dr Martin Brizard founded Travel Health Group.

A family physician since 1987, Dr. Brizard is also the scientific co-director of Colloque Bleu .

In the same vein, Dr. Dominique Tessier, also a family physician, has set up the Colloque Bleu, on the health of international travellers and immunizations.

This annual conference invites healthcare professionals (doctors, nurses, pharmacists) to update their knowledge of travel health and travel immunizations.

The medical team at the Travel Health Group clinic

Always ready to support you in choosing your travel immunizations or obtaining your general vaccinations , our nurses work with respect and attention to detail.

In addition to Drs. Brizard and Tessier, our medical team includes the following nurses: Guylaine Dionne, Daphne Paiva, Danielle Chicoine, Kikalulu Mawa, and Katherine Boivin Scott.
