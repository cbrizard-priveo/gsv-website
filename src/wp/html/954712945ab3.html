It’s a highly contagious viral infection that mainly affects children. The virus is transmitted through contaminated food or water. After multiplying in the intestine, it invades the nervous system.<\/p>\n

In many cases, the infection remains asymptomatic, but affected individuals nevertheless excrete the virus in their faeces and can therefore transmit polio to others.<\/p>\n

Initial symptoms include fever, fatigue, headache, vomiting, stiff neck and aching limbs. In a small number of cases, poliomyelitis leads to paralysis, which is often permanent. Vaccination is the only means of prevention.<\/p>\n

Polio mainly affects children under the age of 5. As there is no cure, prevention is the only option.
