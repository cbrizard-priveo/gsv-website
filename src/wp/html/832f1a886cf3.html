Dukoral\u00ae is a vaccine against traveler’s diarrhea and cholera. It’s an orally administered vaccine that helps protect the body against traveler’s diarrhea caused by E.<\/a> coli enterotoxigenic bacteria (ETEC). Coli enterotoxigenic bacteria (ETEC)<\/a>. E. Coli is the most common cause of travelers’ diarrhea.<\/p>\n

Dukoral\u00ae vaccine consists of two oral doses taken at least one week apart, with the first dose taken at least two weeks before travel.<\/p>\n

For any subsequent trip, if three to five years have passed since your last vaccine dose, a booster dose will renew your protection.<\/p>\n
