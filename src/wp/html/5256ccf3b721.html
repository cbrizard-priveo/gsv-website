Priveo Santé is delighted to announce the opening of its new clinic in Longueuil, located at 5125 Boul Cousineau.
This expansion on Montreal’s South Shore marks an important milestone in our commitment to providing first-rate healthcare services.
We are particularly excited to introduce our specialized travel health offering through Groupe Santé Voyage, recognized as an expert in travel health since 1997.

Specialized travel health services

International travel requires careful preparation to ensure your safety and well-being.
At our new clinic, we offer comprehensive travel health services, backed by the expertise of Groupe Santé Voyage, to prepare you optimally for your international travels.

Here’s what we propose:

  • Personalized travel health consultations: Our team of experienced nurses will provide you with advice tailored to your destination, taking into account the specific health requirements of each country and the potential risks.
  • Required and recommended vaccinations: We offer a full range of vaccinations to protect you against tropical diseases and other potential infections, depending on your destination.
  • Advice on medical preparations: In addition to vaccinations, we provide recommendations on preventive measures, medicines to take with you, and advice in the event of illness during your trip.
  • Vaccination certificates: We issue the necessary certificates for international travel, to meet the requirements of local and international health authorities.

Why choose our travel health services?

  • Groupe Santé Voyage expertise: With over 27 years’ experience, Groupe Santé Voyage is a recognized leader in the field of travel health.
    You benefit from first-rate expertise to ensure you are well prepared and safe for your travels.
  • Convenience and accessibility: Located in the heart of Saint-Hubert in Longueuil, our clinic is easily accessible for South Shore residents.
    We’re committed to providing fast, efficient service to meet your travel health needs.
  • Personalized approach: Every trip is unique.
    We tailor our services and recommendations to your destination, your state of health and your specific needs.

Vaccinations offered by our travel health professionals:

  • Yellow fever
  • Twinrix (Hepatitis A and B)
  • Havrix (Hepatitis A)
  • Engerix (Hepatitis B)
  • Dukoral
  • Vivotif (Typhoid fever)
  • Typhim (Typhoid fever)
  • Ixiario (Japanese encephalitis)
  • Nimemrix (Meningitis)
  • Imovax / Robavert (Rage)
