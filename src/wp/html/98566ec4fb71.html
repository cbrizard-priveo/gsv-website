Are you planning to visit South America soon? You’ll need to make sure your vaccination record is up to date. Whatever country you plan to visit in this part of the world, you need to protect yourself against the risks  could expose you to. Let’s take a look at the mandatory and recommended vaccines before you fly to South America. <\/span><\/p>\n

Mandatory vaccines <\/span><\/h2>\n
    \n
  • Yellow fever vaccine for those planning to visit  in the Amazon and for specific stays in South America. <\/span><\/li>\n<\/ul>\n

    Highly recommended vaccines <\/span><\/h2>\n

    The following vaccines are  strongly recommended, depending on your itinerary, length of stay and travel conditions. <\/span><\/p>\n

      \n
    • Hepatitis A and B vaccines ;<\/span><\/li>\n
    • Rabies vaccine ;<\/span><\/li>\n
    • Typhoid fever vaccine.<\/span><\/li>\n
    • Tetanus vaccine ;<\/span><\/li>\n
    • Measles, mumps and rubella vaccine ;<\/span><\/li>\n<\/ul>\n
