What you need to know about RSV (Respiratory Syncytial Virus)

RSV (Respiratory Syncytial Virus) is responsible for bronchiolitis and pneumonia. It mainly attacks infants and the elderly. The latter are at greater risk of developing severe forms of these illnesses. Health risks associated with RSV In infants, RSV often leads to bronchiolitis. It causes inflammation of the lungs.
