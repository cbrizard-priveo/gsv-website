Flu season is just around the corner. Like every year, we need to prepare ourselves for this period. We need to take all the necessary precautions to protect our health and that of our family. Because, yes, the risk of contracting contagious diseases increases during this period. Among them, we’re all familiar with the flu. <\/span><\/p>\n

Flu and high season <\/span><\/h2>\n

In Canada, the flu season generally runs from December to April. However, we can contract this respiratory virus all year round. The risks are all the greater during the holiday season. As a result, you’re more likely to come into contact with people with respiratory viruses. <\/span><\/p>\n

And yet everyone wants to enjoy these family moments without constraints. We’ll leave aside the various health precautions. <\/span><\/p>\n

The flu virus is transmitted through secretions from the nose and throat. In other words, a person who coughs or sneezes can infect you. You can also catch the disease by touching a contaminated object. <\/span><\/p>\n

The flu vaccine <\/span><\/h2>\n

Unlike the common cold, influenza is a contagious respiratory infection. It causes symptoms of varying severity. These include fever, cough, aches and pains, and throat irritation. We can also experience aches and pains and even intense fatigue. And for several days. <\/span><\/p>\n

Influenza can even lead to hospitalization. This occurs mainly in the elderly. Fortunately, there are now flu vaccines that protect us effectively. We can enjoy them from the age of 6 months. Unless contraindicated, of course. <\/span><\/p>\n

Consult a healthcare professional to find out if your health qualifies. At Clinique Sant\u00e9 Voyage, our general vaccination service includes flu vaccinations. Please do not hesitate to contact us for further information. You can also visit our clinics directly. <\/span><\/p>\n

Important information: we are moving on<\/span>st<\/span> August 2023!<\/span><\/h2>\n

Our new address: 1435 Boul Saint-Martin Ouest local 425, Laval. The clinic is located on the 4th floor. You can take the elevator to get there. and there’s free parking at the rear of the building.<\/span><\/p>\n
