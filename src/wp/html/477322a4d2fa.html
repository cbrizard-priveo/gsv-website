About Travel Health Group

All-in-one travel clinic

One of the first institutions to take an interest in the health of globetrotters, Groupe Santé Voyage is a travel clinic that takes care of the medical planning of your trip.

By providing a range of services such as travel vaccinations, pre-travel medical consultations and prescription medication, we contribute to your peace of mind abroad.
