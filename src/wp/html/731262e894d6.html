Typhoid fever is a potentially fatal infection caused by the Salmonella typhi bacterium.<\/p>\n

It is generally transmitted by drinking contaminated water or eating contaminated food.<\/p>\n

Its main symptoms are prolonged fever, fatigue, headaches, nausea, abdominal pain and transit disorders (constipation or diarrhea). A rash may occur in some patients. Severe cases can lead to serious complications and even death.<\/p>\n

Typhoid fever can be treated with antibiotics, but increasing resistance to different types of antibiotics makes treatment more complex.<\/p>\n

Even after symptoms have disappeared, people can remain carriers of the typhoid bacterium and transmit it via the faecal route.<\/p>\n
