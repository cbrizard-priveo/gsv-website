Traveler’s Diarrhea Vaccination (Dukoral ©)

Dukoral® is a vaccine against traveler’s diarrhea and cholera. It’s an orally administered vaccine that helps protect the body against traveler’s diarrhea caused by E. coli enterotoxigenic bacteria (ETEC). Coli enterotoxigenic bacteria (ETEC). E. Coli is the most common cause of travelers’ diarrhea.

Dukoral® vaccine consists of two oral doses taken at least one week apart, with the first dose taken at least two weeks before travel.

For any subsequent trip, if three to five years have passed since your last vaccine dose, a booster dose will renew your protection.
