European countries are generally considered safe destinations in terms of health safety. Nevertheless, it’s always a good idea to take the necessary steps to prevent certain diseases, especially if you’re a person at risk. Let’s talk about vaccines that might be recommended for a trip to Europe.<\/span><\/p>\n

Are there any compulsory vaccinations for travellers to Europe? <\/span><\/h2>\n

No. However, certain vaccines may be recommended depending on your destination and age group. A travel health consultation will answer all your questions. <\/span><\/p>\n

Opt for our pre-travel vaccination service! <\/span><\/h2>\n

With over 25 years’ experience in the field, Groupe Sant\u00e9 Voyage offers a quality pre-travel vaccination service. With a wide choice of vaccines, including tetanus, polio, diphtheria, childhood diseases, rabies, hepatitis A and B, HPV, whooping cough and yellow fever. <\/span><\/p>\n

Our nurses will draw up a vaccination plan based on your situation and plans: <\/span><\/p>\n

    \n
  • Your state of health<\/span><\/li>\n
  • Your destination and the vaccination regulations of the country concerned;<\/span><\/li>\n
  • The length of your trip. <\/span><\/li>\n<\/ul>\n
