Rabies is a fatal disease caused by a Lyssavirus that is transmitted to humans through close contact with the saliva of an infected animal, most often by biting, scratching or licking.<\/p>\n

Your risk depends on several factors: your destination, the length of your stay, where you travel, your activities and your access to medical care.<\/p>\n

Your risk is much higher if you engage in activities associated with close contact with animals, such as cave exploration, hunting, camping, hiking or cycling.<\/p>\n

Travelers who will be working in close contact with animals, such as veterinarians, animal control officers, wildlife workers and laboratory workers, are at high risk.<\/p>\n
