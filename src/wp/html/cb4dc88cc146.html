World regions and travelers' health

Home > Recommended vaccination by world region

To help you plan your trip, we've compiled a list of pathogens circulating in different parts of the world.

Be prepared, consult a travel health professional quickly when your travel plans take shape. Recommended and required vaccinations, depending on your condition and destination, can be updated with greater flexibility.

Africa

Most African countries require a travel vaccination plan.

In addition, the climate, the presence of insects and local sanitary conditions are more conducive to the spread of pathogens causing :

  • Meningitis
  • Poliomyelitis (Polio)
  • Tetanus
  • Cholera
  • Yellow fever
  • Hepatitis A and B
  • Rabies

To avoid the inconvenience of contracting a virus or bacteria, our health professionals can recommend immunizations tailored to your trip.

South America

Whether it’s relaxing on pristine beaches or immersing yourself in the rich local culture, South America has more than one gem to offer.

Nevertheless, travelers’ health remains an issue. Consult our healthcare professionals for more information on:

  • Yellow fever
  • Hepatitis A and B
  • Tetanus
  • Malaria
  • Typhoid
  • Dengue fever
  • Zika

Asia

From India to Japan to China, Asia is a popular travel destination for Canadians.

Taking preventive measures prior to your trip can help protect you against :

  • Japanese encephalitis
  • Dengue fever
  • Rabies
  • Cholera
  • Malaria
