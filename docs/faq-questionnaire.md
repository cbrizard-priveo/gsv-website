# FAQ — questions for the clinic

These questions are common in travel clinic FAQs, but neither the site nor an official source answers
them for Groupe Santé Voyage. They are **not on the site** until the clinic answers them. Answer in French
(the English is translated from it); short and factual is best.

## Appointments

1. **Walk-ins:** do you take patients without an appointment, or only by appointment?
2. **Vaccines at the consultation:** are the recommended vaccines given during the same visit as the
   consultation?
3. **Length:** how long does a travel consultation usually last?
4. **What to bring:** what should travellers bring (vaccination booklet, itinerary, list of medications…)?
5. **Doctor or nurse:** will the traveller see a doctor or a nurse, and does it depend on the case?
6. **Children:** do you see children and babies? From what age?
7. **Several travellers:** can a family or a couple be seen together in one appointment?

## Fees and payment

8. **Consultation fees:** is there a consultation fee in addition to the vaccines? Is it the same for the
   later doses of a series given at your clinic?
9. **Payment methods:** still Interac, cash, Visa and Mastercard, no cheques? *(already on the site — to
   confirm)*
10. **Insurance:** is there anything to add beyond « not covered by the RAMQ; many private insurers
    reimburse; receipt provided »? *(already on the site — to confirm)*

## Prescriptions and products

11. **Personal prescriptions:** can a traveller renew their usual medications during a travel
    consultation?
12. **Prescriptions for someone absent:** can someone get travel prescriptions for a partner who can't
    attend?
13. **Travel kits:** are kits still sold in the clinics? Which ones? *(already on the site — to confirm)*

## Specific services

14. **Yellow fever:** are your clinics designated yellow fever vaccination centres (which ones)?
15. **Group rabies vaccination:** the current FAQ says group sessions are held every month in Laval at a
    reduced rate. Is that still offered?
16. **Groups and talks:** the current FAQ offers a travel health talk for groups of 10 or more, with
    Véronique Leblanc as contact (1-855-582-2246, info@cliniquesantevoyage.com). Still offered? Which
    contact should the site give? *(on the site now without the name — to confirm)*
17. **Companies:** anything to say about workplace vaccination (the site has a « Vaccination en
    entreprise » page)?
